I reached a point where I was using my phone, without knowing why I was on it.

I tried to reduce my screen time by turning my phone to black and white mode. But I had to turn it off whenever I wanted to actually see what was on my screen, so that didn’t last long. Neither did the other measures I tried.

Then I came across this piece of information, which gave me the utter ick. Hear me out.

---

We’ve all heard about the attention economy and how we are the product, not the user. But did you know why these platforms work so well? It’s because **we are conditioned through variable rewards**.

This means that, for the most part, **the content we’re watching is mildly entertaining at best**. And in the meager remaining time, you come across something actually interesting. We have found gold. Our brains spike with joy and pleasure… and the cycle repeats.

Essentially, we like a little spice in our lives. If rewards are predictable, they are less rewarding. On the contrary, **when rewards are unpredictable, it becomes a miracle when receive them**.

Scientists have found that this phenomenon makes us maintain continuous effort. Furthermore, the unpredictable nature **curtails our capacity to know when to take breaks**, because the reward might come if I swipe just once more. The result? I scroll through reels in bed at night, even when I can barely keep my eyes open.

---

No wonder we feel bad most of the time. Not only did we consume unsatisfying content, but we also saw our day pass with nothing from our TODO list checked. I should have just watched a movie instead.

I find it so gross to realise that we have been treated like lab rats all this time. Now, when I find myself scrolling mindlessly, I think of this, and I leave the app.

What do you think?

---

### Remember
Social media is designed to keep you scrolling, by mixing just enough interesting content to make stopping feel impossible. Set an intention before you open the app, so you stay in control.
